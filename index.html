About Jae Designer

Designer first. AI second.

I’m Lumbani Jere, a Graphic Designer and AI Content Creator from Blantyre, Malawi, and the founder of DSNR, a creative studio that blends traditional design craft with the latest AI production tools.

For more than nine years I have designed brand identities, logos, flyers, social media graphics, and advertising materials, and that foundation shapes everything I create today.

I have worked with brands and businesses from different parts of the world, taking projects from the first brief to final delivery. More recently, I have produced AI-generated video ads, UGC-style content, and animated sequences, and I edit short-form video for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts with strong hooks, tight pacing, captions, and sound design.

Embracing AI after years of doing everything by hand taught me that the best results come from pairing creative instinct with the right tools. If you are looking for visuals that make people stop, look, and feel something, let’s connect.
